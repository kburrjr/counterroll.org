Read the Introduction

From the grant of Genesis to the judgment of Revelation, the sixty-six books of the Bible are one continuous case: the title conveyed in the garden, the breach and the severance at the fall, the statute given at Sinai, the covenant lawsuits of the prophets, the substitution of the party in the Gospels, the opinions of the apostles, and the final judgment entered at the last day. This volume walks the whole canon book by book, and sets each book beside the legal office it performs: trust, conveyance, statute, registry, complaint, decree, opinion, and judgment.
