How the record is kept

Every citation resolves to the instrument itself — the enrolled act, the Code section, the rule, the verse — and each is read at its own text. A Code print is evidence of the act, never the act; the public law leads and the volume-and-page is the office's locator. Where a proposition rests on a passage, the passage is quoted whole.
